About DefensivePositioningPro

Live AI Defensive Intelligence

DefensivePositioningPro is built for coaches who need defensive answers during the game, not after it. It combines live spray chart tracking, previous game history, recent outcomes, and pitch context to support faster defensive decisions.

What Makes It Different

This is not a scorekeeping app and it is not just a static spray chart. DefensivePositioningPro is designed as an AI-native live defensive intelligence tool: the app uses the information coaches enter during gameplay to help identify hitter tendencies and recommend smarter defensive adjustments.

Actual App Features

  • Live spray chart tracking during games
  • Previous-game spray chart history
  • Team spray chart view
  • Current game and previous game views
  • Defensive positioning suggestions based on recorded outcomes
  • Last 5 at-bats display for the active hitter
  • Quick buttons for strikeout, walk, bunt, and undo
  • Pitcher Mode for pitch type and location context
  • Hidden AI reasoning available when prompted

Pitcher Mode

Pitcher Mode is designed to add hitter context, not to be a full pitch analytics platform. Pitch type and location information can help the app better understand hitter tendencies and how outcomes develop.

Coaching First

DefensivePositioningPro supports coaches with live AI-assisted recommendations. Coaches remain responsible for final decisions, player safety, communication, and game management.
