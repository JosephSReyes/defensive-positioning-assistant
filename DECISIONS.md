## Standing Decisions

These apply for the entire project unless explicitly overridden:

- Swipe navigation (Step 10) is **implemented** on `.field-wrap`: swipe right → next batter, swipe left → previous batter. Chevron buttons remain as the accessible fallback.
- Authentication guards (Step 6) should be lightweight. Do not build a custom auth system — use Memberstack's built-in mechanisms (currently the hosted modal triggered by `data-ms-modal` attributes on the landing page).
- AI responses (Steps 8, 9) must be validated and sanitized before being rendered to the DOM.
- Local storage is the only client-side persistence mechanism. Do not introduce IndexedDB or cookies without explicit approval.
- No pitchers feature. Pitcher-related HTML scaffolding in `index.html` is inert — do not activate or remove it without explicit instruction.
- Fielding text is in the **fielder's** perspective. Do not flip it back to the coach's perspective without explicit instruction (see decision 6).
- The **Bunt** quick button (it replaced **HBP**) logs a no-location plate appearance and is excluded from tendency, hot/cold, slump/streak, AI coach-brief context, and at-bat counts — the same treatment as Walk. Do not let bunts feed contact-tendency math without explicit instruction (see decision 9).
- The game screen is **one no-scroll screen** (`#gameScreen.active`/`.game-screen` `height:100dvh`/`100vh` fallback, `overflow:hidden`; `.app` top/bottom padding zeroed for this screen; `.game-screen` bottom padding clears the fixed verse footer). Every `.game-screen` child is `flex-shrink:0` **except** `.field-wrap` (`flex:1 1 0`), which absorbs the leftover space; `.field-wrap` is a size container and the field is the **largest square that fits**: `#gameScreen .field { width:min(100cqw, 100cqh); aspect-ratio:1/1 }`. This keeps the diamond large (fills the width on tall phones) while guaranteeing nothing scrolls and no control is pushed off-screen. **Do not** size the field by leftover height alone (collapses it — decision 10), and **do not** make it a fixed full-width square or switch the screen to scroll (pushes the buttons off-screen — no scroll is a hard requirement). See decisions 10 and 12. `tests/e2e/field-responsive.spec.js` guards no-scroll, no-clip (Run Analysis on-screen), and that the field equals the largest square that fits.
- The game field background is **one SVG drawn in the data's 0–100 coordinate space**, and `#field` is locked to a **fixed (square) aspect ratio** (see decision 11). Do not go back to mixed percentage/pixel/rotation div geometry, and do not let the field take a free-form aspect ratio — the drawing must stay in the data's coordinate space so the diamond, the zone polygon, and the hit dots stay aligned. If the field's proportions or the `zoneLabel` polygon change, change them together. `tests/e2e/field-responsive.spec.js` guards the locked ratio and the data/SVG alignment.
- **Bunt likelihood is passed to the AI coach brief** (decision 9's bunt exclusion from contact-tendency math stays intact). `buildContext()` in `js/ai.js` computes `bunts` (count), `buntRate`, and `buntLikely` (true when 2+ bunts in history OR a bunt in the last 3 plate appearances). When `buntLikely`, the rules-based read (`buildAIExplainText()`) prepends a one-line bunt alert, and the AI prompt (`ai-coach-brief.js`) leads with a bunt warning before any fielder positioning. Threshold is tunable in `ai.js`.
- **Play-log import writes into the existing previous-game model** (`previousEvents` + `outcomes`, `mode:"previous"`) and **must never create a roster player**. Any play that does not confidently match an existing roster player is **dropped** — there is **no "Unknown Player"** bucket, list, or review UI in v1. The `IMPORT PLAY LOG` button is disabled until the roster has ≥1 player. Do not rebuild the team spray chart for this feature; it auto-builds from per-batter data. **Do not use the third-party scorekeeping-app brand name** anywhere that ships (files, identifiers, UI, comments, docs, commits) — use "Play-Log Import." See decision 13 and `PLAY_LOG_IMPORT.md`.
- **Pitch-type tracking is opt-in** (`appData.trackPitch`, defaults `false`). A compact "Pitch: ON/OFF" pill sits in the `.mode-tabs` row. When ON: located contacts show a 5-button pitch row (FB/CU/CH/SL/OT) in the outcome modal; strikeouts/walks show a small pitch modal (same 5 buttons + Skip). The pitch is stored as an optional `pitch` field on the event/outcome. A small pitch tag appears on each Last 5 chip. Pitch data feeds the AI context as `pitchAdvice[]` (per-pitch-type out rate / hit count), gated on 2+ samples. The AI only recommends attack/avoid when it has sufficient data. This is a batter-side scouting feature — it does not violate the "no pitcher features" rule.
